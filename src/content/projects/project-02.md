A clean and balanced interior defined by neutral tones and dark wood elements that structure the space. Light finishes keep the atmosphere calm, while stone surfaces and subtle green accents in the kitchen add depth. The design tries to stay minimal and controlled, with a strong contrast introduced in the bathroom through a bold red ceramic volume, creating a clear focal point within an otherwise restrained composition.
