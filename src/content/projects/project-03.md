A contemporary interior defined by dark walnut volumes and precise detailing, integrating storage and spatial transitions into a unified language. A restrained palette of warm neutrals, natural stone, and soft textures allows light to shape the spaces, while subtle contrasts introduce focus without disrupting cohesion. The project is conceived as a continuous sequence of spaces, emphasizing clarity, material quality, and controlled visual balance.
